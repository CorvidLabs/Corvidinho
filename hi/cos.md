# Chief of staff

## Intent

Corvidinho is chief of staff for the entire team and remembers each person. Leif chose routines first: a daily briefing, sent as a DM to him and to each teammate (#102).

Captured from Leif's 2026-09-28 interview record (round 17, 2026-10-07): COS-1 and COS-2 as drafted in #102, plus COS-2.a. Days with nothing to say are skipped, and only the owner and team get briefings (the #102 scope). Public posts and nightly health reports are out of scope.

## Criteria

- **COS-1**  Every working day it DMs me and each teammate a short briefing: what changed, what's blocked, what needs us, and what it did for us.
- **COS-2**  Each briefing is about that person — their projects, their asks, their reviews — and arrives in their own working hours and timezone.
  - **COS-2.a**  Each person's working hours and timezone come from the declared people list; without them, it uses my timezone and 9am.
